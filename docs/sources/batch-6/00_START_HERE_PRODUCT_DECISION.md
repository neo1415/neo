# Talent Q | Consolidated product decision and launch thesis
**30 October 2026 | Product-definition consolidation | Not production readiness or customer validation**

## The decision in one page
Talent Q remains a **global, AI-native candidate and employer operating experience**, not a stripped-down recruitment application. The full previously approved founder vision survives. To begin implementation without infrastructure hell, prioritize the **Capability-to-Opportunity Loop** as the first revenue-critical vertical slice while preserving the comprehensive product blueprint for later slices.

**Frontier:** "Find the people who can do the work, not merely the people who write the best CVs. Show the proof, close only the gaps, and move the opportunity forward." Commercial version: **Evidence-first hiring, with Q doing the work.** Candidate version: **Show what you can do, even if your CV doesn't.**

**Why this rather than a generic AI recruiter:** The current market already sells AI sourcing, agent-assisted recruitment, adaptive screening, candidate profiles and career advice. The product must unite **job-specific, permissioned proof, recruiter-approved criteria, genuine mutual interest, and full execution** into one workflow, with personal career development between applications. No single one of these is unclaimed IP. A long-lived, trusted participation and evidence network *could become* defensible if adoption proves it.

## Two concrete promises, no jargon
- **Recruiter:** "Tell Q what needs doing. See candidates who demonstrate the skills, including people a keyword search would miss. Q helps you validate, contact, assess and coordinate them." Human review controls hiring decisions.
- **Candidate:** "Show your real capabilities once, choose who sees them, find genuine opportunities, and let Q help you prepare, apply and follow through." No fee merely to apply or become searchable.

## The key product example
An employer requests a backend engineer to troubleshoot PostgreSQL and APIs. **Tobi has a poorly worded CV**, missing many of the exact keywords, but provides a code repository with permission, a product walk-through and a reviewed debugging work sample. **Seyi has a polished CV**, claiming all keywords, but mostly self-reported, outdated and unreviewed evidence.

Q must:
1. Translate the employer's real work into **approved, observable criteria** and what evidence could satisfy each one.
2. Search across permitted talent records using semantic competencies, not CV prose alone. Keyword-match failures must not be hard rejection.
3. Assemble **source-cited evidence** from work samples, verified credentials, permissible project inspections, and candidate claims, with confidence and limits.
4. Rank for **coverage, supported competence and mutual fit separately**, never manufacture a global talent worth score or equate unknown with weak.
5. Show a **"why Tobi may be stronger than his CV"** compare view. For Seyi, plainly say "strong claims, limited supporting evidence." Do not invert the bias by treating weak writing as evidence of superior skill.
6. Ask one targeted follow-up for genuinely unresolved requirements (or human review), not a redundant 90-minute assessment.
7. With permission and approved action, contact and schedule, maintain truthful statuses and keep both sides updated.
8. Route outcomes and candidate-approved feedback into J7 career development and future, consented evidence reuse.

**Unverified claim ≠ incompetence. Demonstrated sample ≠ guaranteed on-the-job performance. Q's analysis ≠ final hiring decision.**

## Decisions now
1. **Brand:** Talent Q (space), gold/near-black/paper, Direction D; do not change these.
2. **Wedge:** Evidence-first, role-specific hiring with less wasted candidate and recruiter effort.
3. **First payer:** frequent-hiring in-house recruiter or specialized recruiting agency. Candidate core remains free.
4. **First role families:** software/backend and data/analyst roles, with transferable ontology design for other professions. This is a first validation focus, not a limitation of Talent Q's eventual coverage.
5. **First distribution:** permitted candidate submissions, employer-owned candidate records, direct applicant signups, optional authorized evidence integrations. Never depend on unauthorized LinkedIn extraction.
6. **Product experience:** Q across every page, proactive but not invasive; chat, voice and forms; one persistent context and explicit permissions; all major actions create truthful artifacts and events.
7. **Execution:** do not rebuild all 21 conceptual modules as separate microservices. Keep coherent modular boundaries; implement one vertical slice against the approved design and shared Q contracts.
8. **Prototype vs implementation:** keep the prototype as the executable UX reference. Production services and persistence are separate and audited. Reuse stable design tokens, components and contracts selectively, not mock business logic.

## Sequence
**P0: freeze and reconcile** (brief). Source inventory, all open decisions, permission/privacy bug checks, Q reuse contract audit.
**P1: one real end-to-end role**. Recruiter requirement → approved rubric → candidate profile and evidence → permissioned retrieval → human-reviewable comparison → consented interest → one real communication channel → interview schedule and truthful pipeline.
**P2: candidate continuous value**. J7 targeted practice and limited assessed proof, CV generation from true claims, relevant jobs and feedback/next steps.
**P3: scale after reliable flows**. Full campaigns/agency, richer evidence types, multiple channels, live interviews, coordinated agents, portability and international specialization.

**Definition of first commercially meaningful outcome:** One verified employer finds and speaks to one consenting candidate because of credible relevant evidence that a resume-only process could plausibly miss, and the hiring process proceeds without manual stitching across apps.
