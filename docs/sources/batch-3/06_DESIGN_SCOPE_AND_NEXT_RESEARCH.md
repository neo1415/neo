# Talent Q | Research backlog, design scope and independent verification

## What is now captured
- The original founder ambition has 32 traceability themes covering one reusable Q, agent/call/email tools, adaptive interviewing, portfolio/skills evidence, candidate growth, flexible modes, premium animation and smart artifacts. The trace register must grow as new requests arrive.
- The atlas enumerates 146 *screen concepts*, not 146 wholly separate interfaces, with common variants and permissions.
- The motion atlas contains 41 animation behaviors with honest backend event triggers, fallback descriptions and recording targets.
- The master research file documents user journeys, architecture/experience constraints and source links.
- The Claude directive converts that material into actual editable design and prototype deliverables.

## Still unknown and must be researched before paid production integrations
1. **LinkedIn or other talent data rights:** exact partner licenses, API eligibility, volume, storage and outreach permissions by region.
2. **Agency/recruiter demand:** field observation of real employers and candidates, actual payment willingness, conversion targets.
3. **Design usability:** direct sessions with at least 5 recruiter archetypes and 5 candidate archetypes, low-bandwidth trials and accessibility verification.
4. **Interview-scoring validity:** role-specific competency anchors, expert panel judgments, inter-rater agreement, subgroup fairness and validated use limitations.
5. **Technical work samples:** specialists' chosen tasks by profession, time burden, accessibility, integration/API economics.
6. **Voice and video terms:** Tavus/ElevenLabs current contract, allowed data, consent rules, per-minute pricing, simultaneous sessions, latency and fallback.
7. **Email/WhatsApp/voice provider restrictions:** sender reputation, opt-in, region, eligibility, recording/transcription restrictions.
8. **On-device browser preview security:** network egress allowlist, anti-SSRF, sandbox, safe downloading, malware handling, public-site terms.
9. **Candidate asset and document handling:** copyright/IP, work-permit sensitive data, expiry and employer revocation.
10. **Privacy law:** jurisdiction-specific recruitment automation, decision rights, data retention, non-discrimination and access requests.
11. **Outcome learning:** defensible labels beyond 'hired' and acceptable practices for model retraining, profile persistence and cross-employer sharing.
12. **True component reuse from Q:** dependency graph on deployed revision, licensing, Q prompts vs generic contracts, extraction regression suite.

## Practical research plan
**Sprint R1: Design source collection.** Capture official UI screenshots and workflows for LinkedIn Recruiter, Greenhouse/MyGreenhouse, Jobberman, MyJobMag, hireEZ/Juicebox, BrightHire/Metaview, CodeSignal/CoderPad/Hack The Box, Teal/Simplify and an accessible digital government or job application service. Label each source as first-party product documentation, independent review, user report or original direct test. Secure permissions for screenshots used in public materials.

**Sprint R2: Real human workflows.** Recruit 5 employers across frequent/occasional hiring and 5 candidates including junior, experienced, passive and low-bandwidth. Record their current flow without prompting; measure elapsed time, dropout, uncertainty, error and motivations. Validate the 8 golden journeys with actual users, not only team members.

**Sprint R3: Interaction research.** Evaluate whether Q should reveal research stages as a timeline, drawer or inline panels. Compare three gold/black designs by task completion, comprehension, accessibility and aesthetics. Test phone first, desktop second, not only hero mockups.

**Sprint R4: Provider feasibility.** For Tavus, ElevenLabs, WhatsApp Business calling, candidate verification and specialist assessments, map capabilities, access, consent, costs and integration constraints. Build one minimal secure adapter trial for chosen provider only with approval.

**Sprint R5: Matching science.** Select 2-3 job families. Define job-related outcomes and work sample rubrics with qualified domain experts. Use publicly permitted/synthetic evidence for early development. No protected-attribute ranking; test adverse impact as part of validation.

**Sprint R6: Technical reuse audit.** Compare Q on current deployed/integration refs, get bounded context/port ownership and security data, create a proof that Talent Q uses the shared Q run without importing Capital Q tenant/domain state. Maintain existing Q integration branch release discipline.

## User-approved aesthetic direction
- Product name: **Talent Q**.
- Gold is Q. Black/warm white are support. Avoid gradients unless justified.
- Film: 4m33s dark/premium reference with Q gold orb, spare serif narratives, split desktop/mobile and gradual source/result reveals.
- Real product improves small text in film: legible default, accessible contrast, reduced motion and very fast first interaction.
- No fabricated progress states or fake search counts, even when cinematic.
